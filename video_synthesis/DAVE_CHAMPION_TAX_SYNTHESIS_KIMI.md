# Dave Champion on the Federal Income Tax — A Synthesis of 33 Video Transcripts

*A cross-reading of the 33 tax videos in the [income-tax index](https://dogthathunts.github.io/transcript-summaries/dave-champion-income-tax.html), built to surface where Champion's arguments **reinforce** each other and where they **conflict**. Each finding is tagged with the video(s) it draws from and a confidence label. Analysis is grounded in the transcripts themselves, not the AI summaries.*

**Confidence:** **High** = same statute/claim stated two opposing ways, quotable both sides · **Medium** = real tension, partly reconcilable as emphasis/context · **Low** = minor or likely wording/ASR artifact. Where a claim conflicts with an *external* authority he cites (not with himself), it is labelled **[claim vs. source]**.

**Fidelity caveat:** the analysis below is grounded in raw ASR transcripts (unreliable punctuation, occasional mis-hearings — e.g. "Brushaber" rendered as "Bruce Schaefer"). A flagged contradiction could be substantive or an artifact of ASR/summary; it is labelled accordingly.

---

## The central thesis (what every video is arguing)

One claim underlies all 33: **the federal income tax, as actually written, was never imposed on ordinary Americans earning domestic income in the fifty states** — they pay only out of ignorance or fear induced by a decades-long government "disinformation campaign." What differs video-to-video is the *route* he takes to that conclusion, and those routes are the source of most of the inconsistencies below.

He builds the thesis on four sub-argument pillars, used in varying combination:

- **P1 Definitional** — statutory terms of art ("employee", "wages", "includes", "U.S. person", "trade or business", "state") are narrow and exclude ordinary Americans.
- **P2 Forms/withholding** — [W-4](https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-form-w-4)/[W-9](https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-form-w-9)/[1040](https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-form-1040)/[1099](https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-form-1099) apply only to the federally-connected; signing them self-inflicts a false legal presumption.
- **P3 Constitutional** — the income tax is an *excise on a privilege*; labor and property are rights and cannot be taxed directly without apportionment.
- **P4 Jurisdiction/enforcement** — "state"/"United States" mean D.C./territories; the IRS enforces by bluff, hidden Treasury Decisions, and computer presumptions.

## Where the argument reinforces itself (high agreement across videos)

These claims recur near-verbatim across many videos and form the load-bearing spine:

- **The W-9 is a "withholding certificate" whose *sole* purpose is to certify, under penalty of perjury, that you hold a foreign person's U.S.-source income as their domestic agent.** The single most-repeated claim in the corpus. *(see: 1217, 1219, 1220, 1225, 1226, 1232, 1236, 1239, 1241, 1243, 1247, 1249, 1250)*
- **Form 1040 is only for non-resident aliens / foreign corporations or their domestic agents, per a run of Treasury Decisions since 1913 — none of which names an ordinary American.** *(see: 1217, 1218, 1221, 1226, 1227, 1236, 1237, 1239, 1245, 1247, 1249, 1252, 1254)*
- **The statutory "employee" ([§3401(c)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/3401)) means only government officers/employees — private-sector workers cannot be read in — via the "includes/means" term-of-art rule.** *(see: 1219, 1222, 1231, 1234, 1241, 1244, 1246, 1254)*
- **Signing a federal tax form is the self-inflicted act that creates liability** — the presumption is manufactured by your own signature, not imposed by statute. This is the connective tissue of the whole system. *(see: 1217, 1218, 1223, 1227, 1232, 1236, 1237, 1243, 1255)*
- **The income tax is a constitutional excise on a government-granted privilege; earning a living and owning property are rights, so they can't be taxed directly.** *(see: 1218, 1224, 1235, 1236, 1249, 1252, 1253)*
- **Remedy: read the book first, then rebut every form and build an evidentiary record — never "go off half-cocked."** *(see: 1221, 1223, 1226, 1231, 1235, 1237, 1239, 1243, 1249, 1250, 1252, 1255)*
- **Personal proof-of-concept: he has not filed or paid in ~31–32 years (since 1993).** *(see: 1223, 1225, 1233, 1250, 1253)*

*Structural note:* reinforcement here is largely by **restatement** — the videos share a fixed opening thesis and biographical hook. He also presents his two independent derivations (P1 definitional and P3 constitutional-excise) as *mutually confirming*, though they are logically distinct paths to the same endpoint.

## Inconsistencies & contradictions

**1. The withholding class is defined two incompatible ways. [High]**
The same [§3402](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/3402) / Chapter 24 wage-withholding regime is said to reach **"only government employees"** (via §3401(c)) in some videos and **"only non-resident aliens with U.S.-source wages"** in others. Row 1246 and row 1247 make the *opposing* claims about the *identical* statute. He occasionally stacks both as alternative bases (1241 layers government-employee *and* NRA) but never says which is operative. This is the deepest internal fault line.
*(govt-employee: 1219, 1222, 1231, 1234, 1241, 1244, 1246, 1254 — vs — NRA/foreign-person: 1217, 1236, 1237, 1239, 1247, 1249, 1252)*

**2. "Only two classes — no third class," then a third (and fourth) class. [Medium-High]**
Rows 1236 and 1239 assert flatly that the tax reaches "only two classes… no third class," then in the next breath describe the U.S.-person/withholding-agent as a third role. Separately, the "U.S. citizen abroad with foreign income" class is *conceded* in 1221, 1226, 1227 and 1249 but *omitted* from the "only two" videos. Partly a taxpayer-vs-collector distinction, but stated as a bare contradiction in wording. *(see: 1236, 1239 vs. 1221, 1226, 1227, 1249)*

**3. The Treasury-Decision count drifts. [Medium]**
The number of TDs supposedly restricting Form 1040 to non-resident aliens climbs: **7** *(1217)* → **9** *(1218)* → "9 or 10" *(1219, 1227)* → **11** *(1236, 1237, 1239, 1245, 1247, 1252, 1254)*. Row 1249 partly resolves this ("8 when I wrote the book, now 11"), but 1217 and 1218 sit adjacent in the series at 7 vs. 9. Low-stakes on its own, yet it undercuts the "100% consistent, I've read every one" framing.

**4. "includes" is applied in whichever direction the conclusion needs. [Medium]**
He defines "includes" as a *narrowing* "term of limited expansion" *(1219, 1222, 1231, 1234)* — but in 1244 uses the same word *expansively* to sweep all branches of government into the I-9 "entity" definition ("the entire federal government, without exception"). The interpretive engine bends to the required result. *(see: 1231, 1244)*

**5. Enforcement is "pure bluster" — except when it isn't. [Medium]**
Row 1223 frames IRS enforcement as intimidation that "evaporates when you know the law," yet the same video (and 1249) warns "don't ever, ever try this without study," notes he was "one of the 10 most wanted," and concedes people who stop filing get IRS letters for prior years. Confidence asymmetry runs through the corpus: the *statutory* claims are stated as absolute, while the ~22-insider "cabal" *(1235)*, the "mainframes coded to the truth" theory *(1245)*, and crypto enforcement (which he admits in 1219 he doesn't know is "actually factual," but asserts flatly in 1239) are speculation. *(see: 1223, 1249, 1235, 1245, 1219, 1239)*

**6. He concedes the classic protester arguments are false — while landing on the same bottom line. [Medium]**
Rows 1227 and 1249 explicitly agree with the courts that "person excludes natural persons," "wages aren't taxable," and "filing is voluntary" are **false/frivolous** — distancing himself from Schiff, the §861 position (Snipes), and Tedder. Yet his own conclusion ("no law requires you to pay one red cent") is the same endpoint those "false" arguments reach; the distinction rests entirely on his form-signing theory. *(see: 1227, 1249, 1230)*

**7. [*Moore v. United States*](https://www.law.cornell.edu/supremecourt/text/22-800) is read against itself. [Medium-High — claim vs. source]**
He cites *Moore* (2024) as confirming the income tax is an avoidable excise and that ordinary Americans owe nothing *(1243, 1249, 1252)*, quoting the syllabus line "taxes on income are indirect taxes." But the on-screen text in row 1252 actually displays "Taxes on income are **direct** taxes" — a direct contradiction he verbally inverts to "indirect." *Moore* actually **upheld** the tax against the taxpayers, and row 1252 mis-names it "Moore v. Hooks v. United States." Row 1252 also calls Moore nation-changing while admitting it "simply restates" 1916 [*Brushaber*](https://www.law.cornell.edu/supremecourt/text/240/1) — i.e., nothing new. *(see: 1252, 1249, 1243)*

**8. Sales tax: doesn't apply to him, yet he pays it. [Low-Medium]**
Row 1233 argues sales tax can't touch his selling activity, but his own worked pricing example has him **paying** sales tax on wholesale purchases — implicitly conceding that transaction is taxable. His sole-proprietor "it's a right" logic is also never reconciled with his stated basis for the tax (a *foreign/out-of-state corporation's* "doing-business" privilege), which would still reach in-state incorporated small businesses. *(see: 1233)*

**9. Social Security: "just as much a scam" but reached a different way. [Low-Medium]**
Row 1253 equates FICA with the income tax ("just as much a scam," quoting [*Stewart Machine*](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/301/548/) calling it "merely an additional income tax"), yet argues it via a *jurisdictional* route ("state" = federal places) plus excise-on-privilege — not the foreign-person/U.S.-source-income engine he uses for the income tax. It approvingly quotes *Stewart Machine*'s "additional income tax" line while elsewhere insisting FICA was never imposed on ordinary workers at all. *(see: 1253)*

**10. "No law exists" vs. "no one has found it in 112 years." [Low]**
Row 1246 refuses to assert the authorizing withholding statute *doesn't exist* (only that no one has located it) — then states flatly "there is no law." An epistemic hedge that collapses into the absolute it just declined to make. *(see: 1246)*

## The pattern underneath

The corpus is internally consistent on its **conclusions** and its **most-cited artifacts** (W-9, Form 1040, §3401(c), the Treasury Decisions) but inconsistent on the **mechanism** — it reaches "you owe nothing" by at least four distinct routes (foreign-person, government-employee, territorial-jurisdiction, constitutional-excise) that are presented interchangeably and, in the §3402 case, are mutually exclusive. Certainty tracks argument type: **definitional/statutory claims are absolute; enforcement, motive, and technology claims are openly speculative**; and the affirmative "how-to" is almost always deferred to the paid book. The strongest single thread — repeated in a dozen videos and never contradicted — is the narrow, specific claim that **the W-9 is a withholding certificate about a foreign person's money**; the widest fault line is **who wage-withholding actually applies to.**
